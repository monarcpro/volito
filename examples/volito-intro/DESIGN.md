# Volito

The brand of Volito by MONARC PRO, the motion graphics engine for developers and AI agents. A warm paper
ground (#F4F1EA), near-black ink (#15140F), an electric cobalt (#2433E8) for the one thing that matters and
an acid lime (#DFFF4F) for the call to action; a small orange (#FF6A3D) square lives in the mark. Bricolage
Grotesque at 800 for display, Inter for text, Space Mono for code and tracked labels, Caveat for a
handwritten aside. Corners at 22 px, flat surfaces, hairline ink borders. Motion is snappy: springs that
settle fast, nothing floats.
